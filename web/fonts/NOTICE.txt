Asana-Math.otf
--------------
Vendored so the application does not depend on the font being installed locally.

Source:   https://mirrors.ctan.org/fonts/Asana-Math/Asana-Math.otf  (CTAN)
Project:  https://ctan.org/pkg/asana-math
Author:   Apostolos Syropoulos
Licence:  SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1)
          Full text: https://openfontlicense.org/

The OFL text is not reproduced here because the copy served alongside the font on
CTAN returned 404 at the time of vendoring; consult the link above for the
authoritative terms before redistributing.

Used for numerals, units and mathematical symbols throughout the interface. The
font carries an OpenType MATH table, so it also renders the operators and
relations that appear in the derivation steps.
